- **Rider.** `rider.dashboard`, `rider.availability`, `rider.delivery-requests`, `rider.earnings.index`, `rider.history.index`, and assignment-scoped `fulfillments.index`. Application entry is buyer-gated. [Rider dispatch routes](../routes/web.php#L267) · [Rider portal](../routes/web.php#L296) · [Assignment scope](../app/Http/Controllers/FulfillmentController.php#L23)
- **Logistics.** `admin.logistics.index` and `admin.logistics.update` require an admin. Internal assignments and retry/return controls also use `fulfillments.index` and `fulfillments.update`. [Admin logistics routes](../routes/web.php#L232) · [Fulfillment routes](../routes/web.php#L269)
- **Admin.** `admin.dashboard`, `admin.operations`, `admin.buyers.index`, `admin.partners.index`, `admin.security.*`, `admin.categories.*`, `admin.products.index`, `admin.reviews.index`, and seller/rider approval routes.
